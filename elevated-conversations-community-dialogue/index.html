Elevated Conversations: Redefining the Community Dialogue Around Cannabis

In a world where conversations about cannabis often skim the surface, we believe in going deeper. Elevated Conversations is our way of fostering thought-provoking, insightful, and engaging discussions that go beyond the ordinary. Whether it's about the history of cannabis, industry best practices, cultural perceptions, or personal wellness, we're here to spark meaningful dialogue and challenge perspectives.

What is Elevated Conversations?

Elevated Conversations is a monthly series held on the first Sunday of each month where we dive into ideas that shape the way we think, feel, and interact with cannabis. Inspired by the philosophy behind By Any Other Name, we embrace nuance, curiosity, and open-minded discussion, because names, labels, and perceptions don’t define the full story.

From exploring the historical roots of cannabis use to discussing modern legalization, best practices for responsible consumption, and the science behind its benefits, our conversations are designed to inspire and educate. We invite our community to share their experiences, exchange ideas, and question the status quo.

Why It Matters

Now more than ever, intentional cannabis conversations are necessary. Whether it’s reimagining how we connect with the plant, reshaping cultural narratives, or advocating for inclusivity in the industry, Elevated Conversations aims to bridge gaps and build understanding. It’s about creating a space where authenticity thrives and new perspectives are welcomed.

Join the Conversation

We want to hear from you. What cannabis-related topics resonate with you? What conversations need to be had? Follow us on Instagram and Facebook and drop a comment, or visit our store to experience the dialogue in person. Because when we elevate the conversation, we elevate the experience.

Stay tuned for upcoming features, guest perspectives, and special events that bring these discussions to life. Elevated Conversations is a monthly series, don’t miss out! Sign up for our mailing list to receive updates, and check our blog regularly for announcements on new topics and special guests.

Let’s keep the dialogue going, one Elevated Conversation at a time.
