# By Any Other Name: redesign preview

A redesign concept for By Any Other Name, the licensed cannabis dispensary at 946 Fulton Street, Brooklyn. This
repository holds the built static site only. It is a preview for review, served with `noindex` on every page.

- Preview: served by GitHub Pages from this repository
- Live site it is based on: https://byanyothernamebk.com/

## Design

Feminine, clean and luxurious, on the brand's own lilac, oxblood and pearl: Bodoni Moda for display type with Jost for
text, frosted glass, framed pictures that cross the seams between sections, and generous white space.

Motion, all without libraries and all switched off for visitors who ask for reduced motion:

- the home page opens with the brand's wordmark as a window onto the greenhouse film, then zooms through the T of
  OTHER until the film fills the screen and the centred headline and buttons arrive (once per visit);
- the film recedes into a rounded print as you scroll on;
- section headings condense letter by letter, text arrives from a soft blur, pictures open with a curtain;
- a pinned spotlight reel walks through the five featured products, each opening from a slit;
- a pinned macro of the flower opens to full bleed;
- count-ups, a two-way marquee with a pause control, and a quick-action bar that slides up after the hero;
- magnetic buttons with a sheen, category cards that lift and tilt while the others dim, menus that wipe open.

## What is in it

- The live site's pages at their own addresses: the shop (the store's existing online menu embed), contact,
  accessibility, privacy, terms, the Elevated Conversations post with its event, tag and archive pages, and the five
  product pages.
- New pages written only from the store's published facts: a home page, About, Visit, Pickup and delivery, and FAQ.
- Structured data (Organization, Store with opening hours, breadcrumbs), Open Graph and Twitter tags, a sitemap index
  and an llms.txt, all naming the live addresses.
- A 21+ age check, live "open now" status in New York time from the published hours, and the store's own warning text.

## Imagery

The logo, wordmark, monogram, botanical illustration, store interior, event photo and product photos are the client's
own. These pictures are AI-generated, marked `data-ai="1"` in the markup and AI-labelled in their files: the greenhouse
hero still and its looping film, the rose and cannabis bloom, the cannabis herbarium page, the salon table, the pickup
bag, the jars of flower, the trichome macro, the leaf canopy, the three category pictures (flower, pre-rolls,
vaporizers) and a paper texture.

## Not connected in this preview

The sign-up and contact forms do not send anything. The fonts are open-licence stand-ins for the brand's own faces.
