# By Any Other Name: redesign preview

A redesign concept for By Any Other Name, the licensed cannabis dispensary at 946 Fulton Street, Brooklyn. This
repository holds the built static site only. It is a preview for review, served with `noindex` on every page.

- Preview: served by GitHub Pages from this repository
- Live site it is based on: https://byanyothernamebk.com/

## Design: "Quiet Bloom"

Frosted glass, a luxury serif and mono type pairing, and wabi-sabi materials (lime plaster, handmade paper, stoneware,
linen, travertine, leaf shadow), on the brand's own lilac, oxblood and paper. Framed pictures cross the seams between
bands for depth; sections reveal as they scroll; buttons, cards, frames and links each have their own hover.

## What is in it

- The live site's pages at their own addresses: the shop (the store's existing online menu embed), contact,
  accessibility, privacy, terms, the Elevated Conversations post with its event, tag and archive pages, and the five
  product pages.
- New pages written only from the store's published facts: a home page, About, Visit, Pickup and delivery, and FAQ.
- Structured data (Organization, Store with opening hours, breadcrumbs), Open Graph and Twitter tags, a sitemap index
  and an llms.txt, all naming the live addresses.
- A 21+ age check, live "open now" status in New York time from the published hours, and the store's own warning text.

## Imagery

The logo, wordmark, monogram, botanical illustration, store interior, event photo and product photos are the client's
own. These pictures are AI-generated and marked `data-ai="1"` in the markup and AI-labelled in their files: the home
hero still and its looping video, the pressed rose and leaf, the salon, the parcel, the brownstone street, the three
category pictures (flower, pre-rolls, vaporizers), and two textures.

## Not connected in this preview

The sign-up and contact forms do not send anything. The fonts are open-licence stand-ins for the brand's own faces.
